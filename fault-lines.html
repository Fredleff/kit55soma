The editorial standard

Provocation without theater.

01

State the tension honestly.

No straw men. The opposing case must be strong enough that a serious operator would recognize it as their own.

02

Follow the consequence.

The argument must eventually land in margin, schedule, risk, capacity, customer trust, or accountability.

03

Remain operationally neutral.

KIT55 Soma does not join a camp for attention. We identify what has to be true for the enterprise outcome to hold.
